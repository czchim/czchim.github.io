Hi, and thank you for your interest. I am a PhD candidate at the [Department of Political Science](https://polisci.ucsd.edu/) and the [School of Global Policy and Strategy](https://gps.ucsd.edu/) of University of California, San Diego. 
My research interests lie in energy and environmental politics, political economy, and development. I study how large-scale infrastructure and energy investment reshape domestic politics and international alignment in the Global South, with a focus on the political consequences of long-term dependence on external funders. Currently, my projects look into Chinese hydropower projects in Southeast Asia.
I received my B.A. in Economics and B.A. in International Affairs from the University of Nevada, Reno with a minor in Mathematics. I received my A.A. in Business Administration from Truckee Meadows Community College. I previously worked on projects about opioid prescribing laws in Nevada and the intermittency challenge of wind energy. 
I was born and raised in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, and I am a first-generation college student. My favorite part of town is the coffee shops on Petaling Street, and my comfort meal is a warm bowl of [Asam Laksa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laksa#Malaysia).
